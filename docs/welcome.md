# 👋 Welcome to ZAMA DAO

Welcome to the official documentation for ZAMA DAO, the next-generation privacy-preserving decentralized governance platform.

## What is ZAMA DAO?
ZAMA DAO empowers communities to make decisions on-chain with true privacy. By leveraging cutting-edge cryptography—specifically Fully Homomorphic Encryption (FHE)—ZAMA DAO ensures that every vote remains confidential, secure, and tamper-proof until the final tally is revealed.

## Our Mission
Our mission is to enable fair, transparent, and private governance for all. We believe that privacy is essential for honest participation and that decentralized organizations deserve the highest standards of security and usability.

## Why ZAMA DAO?
- **Private Voting:** Your vote is encrypted and never revealed individually.
- **On-Chain Security:** All logic is enforced by smart contracts, with no trusted third parties.
- **Open & Inclusive:** Anyone can propose, vote, and participate—without fear of coercion or surveillance.
